For students

The work is yours. So is its history.

Your edits and revisions deserve a record. Provenance preserves supported development activity without watching the rest of your digital life.

Recorded

  • Edits and pastes in the assignment workspace
  • File activity and editor focus
  • Supported terminal commands
  • Installed editor extensions

Never recorded

  • Your browser, screen, camera, or other projects
  • System-level keystrokes
  • Clipboard contents unless pasted into your work
  • Offline. No network requests while recording.
  • Readable. Plain-text logs stay local until submission.
  • Course-defined retention. Your school sets the deletion schedule.
